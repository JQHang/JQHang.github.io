I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Rutgers University, advised by Prof. Desheng Zhang. I work on mining billion-scale heterogeneous graphs built from enterprise data — turning data warehouse tables into graphs and learning over them at scale. The frameworks I build are deployed nationwide at JD Logistics, where they rank hundreds of millions of customers and mine 1.8 billion company–person relations. I am currently developing JoinMiner, an open framework for join-based graph learning.

Projects

JoinMiner

An open framework for large-scale heterogeneous tabular data mining. It turns enterprise data warehouse tables into heterogeneous temporal hypergraphs and learns over them with graph neural networks, built on PySpark for billion-row workloads and driven entirely by YAML configuration.

Key-Contact Mining at Billion Scale BiLink

Mines reliable indirect relations between companies and individuals across billions of records, then predicts which contact to reach out to. A bidirectional sampling strategy makes long-distance path search 13× faster. Deployed nationwide, it surfaced 1.8 billion company–person pairs and raised contact success rate by 86% over the previous expert-designed rules.

High-Potential Customer Discovery Complex-path

Ranks hundreds of millions of customers by potential value using path-based signals over a graph of customers, companies, industries, and regions. Deployed nationwide to prioritize outreach, improving contact success rate by 252% over the previous approach.
